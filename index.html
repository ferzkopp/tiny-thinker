Welcome!

Tiny Thinker runs a small AI language model right inside this browser. Your questions are not sent to an AI server. Getting started takes three steps: set up this device, choose an experiment, then chat and learn by trying questions and comparing replies.

Why it matters?

    What am I picking?

    The engine is the program that runs the model. The model is the AI itself: bigger models usually give better answers, but take longer to download and need more memory.
