Meet Tiny Thinker

AI is showing up in school, work and everyday life. Knowing how to question AI-generated answers matters just as much as knowing how to use AI tools.

Here, you can try a small AI language model: a program that learned patterns from text and uses them to write replies. It runs right in your browser. It's free to use, no account is needed, and your questions are not sent to an AI server.

Choose from experiments, like remixing a story, investigating a surprising answer, comparing languages or drawing with code. Change your question or try different settings, such as the reply length limit or how randomly the AI chooses words, and compare the answers.

AI can sound confident and still be wrong. Practise checking its answers, spotting unfair assumptions and deciding what to keep or improve.

Remember, you are in charge of the thinking.

Your AI playground
